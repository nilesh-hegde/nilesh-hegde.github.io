I am a Predoctoral Research Professional at the Fama-Miller Center for Research in Finance at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, where I work with Ralph Koijen, George M. Constantinides, and Taisiya Sikorskaya. My research interests are in machine learning in finance.

I completed an MS in Computer Science at the Khoury College of Computer Sciences, Northeastern University, where I worked with Carla Brodley and Felix Muzny at the Center for Inclusive Computing on NLP/ML and Economics of (CS) Education.

I received a BTech in Computer Science and Engineering from B.M.S. College of Engineering, Bengaluru. During my undergraduate studies, I worked on algorithmic graph theory with Suresh M. Hegde at NIT Surathkal.

Finance Research

Working Papers

Carbon Uncertainty and Asset Prices
Cathy Yi-Hsuan Chen, George M. Constantinides, Nilesh Hegde, and Abraham Lioui
Measurement uncertainty in firms' carbon intensity commands a positive risk premium, distinct from the premium on carbon-intensity levels. We construct an observation-level measure of carbon-data precision for the full CRSP universe and find that investors price not only carbon exposure but also carbon opacity.
BFI Working Paper, May 2026

Too Easy to Survive: Machine Learning and the Decay of Return Predictability
Nilesh Hegde
Decomposes machine-learning return predictions into linear, pairwise, and neural-network complexity tiers, and studies which layers retain economic value as markets evolve. Structural compressibility forecasts which characteristics lose predictive content over time.
